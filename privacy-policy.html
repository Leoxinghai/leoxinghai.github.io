1. Information We Collect

We ourselves do not collect or store personal information on our own servers. We do not operate a backend that identifies you. The information involved in the Game falls into these categories:

a. Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs

To show ads and process purchases, third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically collect technical information, which can include:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / App Set ID).
  • Device and technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, country/region, mobile network or general (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app-interaction events needed to serve and measure ads.
  • Diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data, where applicable.

b. Purchase information

When you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the transaction. We receive a confirmation that a purchase occurred so we can grant the corresponding in-game item, but we do not receive or store your payment card number or billing details.

c. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us directly (for example, by email for support), we will have whatever information you include in that message. We use it only to respond to you.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

Your game progress — such as current level, coins, energy, hero health, unlocked levels, and settings — is saved locally on your device (in the app's private storage). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may display banner, interstitial (full-screen), and rewarded video ads, and may use your device's advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure those ads.

Personalized vs. non-personalized ads

Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based only on context and coarse signals). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's consent mechanism (the User Messaging Platform) to request your consent before serving personalized ads, as required by law.

How you can control ad tracking

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your Advertising ID and to opt out of ad personalization.
  • Where a consent prompt is shown in the Game, you can decline personalized ads there.

To learn how Google uses the information it collects, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob & privacy information.
